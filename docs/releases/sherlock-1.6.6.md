# EWork Odyssey 1.6.6

适用于 Apple Silicon Mac。

## 中文

- 图像画布框选多个元素后，可通过右键编组、取消编组、复制、删除，或将选中的内容下载为 PNG、JPG。编组后可整体缩放。

- 工具栏新增画布比例菜单，提供带图示的常用比例。品牌资产可以拖出画布边缘，继续在画布外编辑。

- 模板画廊上移预览并使用中性色切换按钮。编辑器底栏与顶栏同色，图层和品牌资产侧栏采用内缩的标题分隔线。

- 消息入口以红点提示未读，消息中心以蓝点标识未读条目。收起左侧栏后，下载或更新按钮与消息入口留出更多间距。

- 修复内置浏览器右侧对话的斜杠子菜单透出网页的问题，长列表可在菜单内滚动。

## English

- Right-click a multi-selection in Images to group, ungroup, copy, delete, or download the selected content as PNG or JPG. Resize grouped layers together.

- Switch canvas aspect ratios from the toolbar using illustrated presets. Drag brand assets outside the canvas and continue editing them there.

- Template previews sit higher and the gallery toggle uses neutral colors. The editor footer matches the toolbar, and Layers and Brand Assets have inset header dividers.

- A red dot marks unread notifications in the sidebar, and blue dots mark unread entries in Notification Center. The download and update button has more space above the bell in the collapsed sidebar.

- Fix webpage content showing through slash-command submenus in the browser side chat. Long lists now scroll inside the submenu.

## 发布范围

桌面版纳入已验收的通知、图像应用和内置浏览器修改。独立网页端的源码面向另一个仓库和部署环境，原分支保留。
